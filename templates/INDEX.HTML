ABOUT THE SYSTEM

One connected system for the entire department.

The Student Management System brings students, teachers, HODs and administrators together in one platform for managing academic information, attendance, marks, department data and leave applications.

01

Student & Department Management

Manage students, teachers, departments, subjects and semester-wise academic data through a centralized system.

02

Academic Management

Students can view attendance and marks while teachers can update academic records for their assigned subjects.

03

Leave & HOD Approval

Students and teachers can apply for leave, while the department HOD can review, approve or reject requests.
